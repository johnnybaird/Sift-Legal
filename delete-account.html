Delete your account

Last updated: 1 October 2026

You can delete your Sift account and the personal information attached to it at any time. There are two ways to do it.

If you can still sign in

This is the fastest way, and the deletion happens immediately.

  1. Open the Sift app and sign in
  2. Go to the Account tab, then Settings
  3. Scroll to the bottom and tap Delete my account
  4. Confirm when prompted

If you can't sign in

Email Siftapp.contact55@gmail.com from the email address registered to your Sift account, with the subject line Delete my account.

We can only act on requests sent from the registered address — this is how we confirm the request is genuinely yours. If you no longer have access to that email account, tell us and we will ask you for other information to verify your identity before deleting anything.

We aim to action requests within a few days, and in any case within 30 days of receiving them.

What gets deleted

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile — name, university, date of birth, biography, phone number, sizes and preferences
  • All of your listings
  • All photographs you have uploaded
  • Your messages
  • Your offers, saved items and blocks
  • Your login credentials

What is kept, and why

  • Messages you sent to other people may remain visible in their copy of the conversation, in the same way an email you have sent stays in the recipient's inbox.
  • Reviews you wrote about other users remain on their profile. Removing them would misrepresent that person's transaction history to people deciding whether to trade with them.
  • Reports made about your account are retained so that we can investigate safety matters and prevent someone from evading a suspension by deleting and recreating an account.
  • Records we are legally required to keep, or that are needed to resolve a dispute or prevent fraud, are retained for as long as the law requires.
  • Encrypted backups may contain your data for a limited period before they expire in the normal course.

Deleting your account cannot be undone. If you sign up again later with the same email address, it will be a new account with none of your previous listings, messages or reviews.

Questions

If you are unsure about any of this, email Siftapp.contact55@gmail.com before deleting. Our Privacy Policy explains in more detail what we collect and how long we keep it.
