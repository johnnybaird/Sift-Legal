2.3 Photographs

When you upload a photo to a listing, the image file is stored on our servers and made accessible via a public web address so it can be displayed in the App.

Every photograph is automatically checked for prohibited content before it is stored. This check is carried out by a third-party image analysis service, which receives a copy of the image for that purpose (see section 5).

Photographs taken on a smartphone can contain embedded metadata, which may include the date, device details, and the GPS coordinates of where the photo was taken. Sift automatically removes this metadata from every photograph before it is stored, so uploaded images do not carry the location of where they were taken.

We still recommend photographing items in neutral or public settings rather than inside your home, since the visible content of a photograph can itself reveal where you live.

4. What Other Users Can See

Understanding this is important, so we set it out plainly.

Visible to any signed-in user:

  • Your display name
  • Your university or campus
  • Your biography
  • Your listings, including all photographs and descriptions
  • Reviews and ratings you have received, and reviews you have written (shown alongside your display name)

Visible only to users you interact with directly:

  • Messages you exchange with them
  • Offers you send or receive

Never visible to other users:

  • Your email address
  • Your phone number
  • Your date of birth
  • Your clothing sizes
  • Your country and currency settings
  • Reports you submit (these are visible only to you and to us)
  • Users you have blocked, and users who have blocked you

Please note: anything you voluntarily type into a public field — such as your biography, a listing description, or a message — is visible to the people who can see that field. Do not include your home address, financial details, identification documents, or other sensitive information in these fields.

7. How We Protect Your Information

We take the security of your information seriously and apply the following measures:

  • Encryption in transit. All communication between the App and our servers uses HTTPS/TLS.
  • Password security. Passwords are hashed using industry-standard algorithms. We cannot recover or view your password. Passwords must be at least 8 characters.
  • Row-level access controls. Our database enforces per-user access rules so that, for example, only the participants in a conversation can retrieve its messages, and only you can read your own profile record.
  • Least-privilege database permissions. Application database roles are granted only the specific permissions required to operate, and unauthenticated access to user data is disabled entirely.
  • Scoped file storage. Photograph uploads and deletions are restricted so that each user can only write to and delete from their own storage area.
  • Location metadata removal. GPS and other metadata is stripped from every uploaded photograph before storage.
  • Verification codes. Signing up and resetting a password require a code sent to your registered email address.

No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. You are responsible for keeping your password confidential and for notifying us promptly at Siftapp.contact55@gmail.com if you believe your account has been compromised.

Data breaches. If a breach occurs that is likely to result in serious harm to you, we will notify you and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner as required under the Notifiable Data Breaches scheme.

8. How Long We Keep Your Information

Please note: messages you have sent may remain visible to the recipient, and reviews you have written about other users may remain on their profile, as removing them would misrepresent that user's transaction history.

See our account deletion page for how to delete your account and exactly what is removed.
